About us

A pet-care partner you can actually rely on.

PawPeer is a pet-care marketplace that helps pet parents find trusted sitters, walkers, and caregivers nearby. We combine simple booking tools, identity verification, and secure payments to make pet care feel safer and more transparent for everyone.

  • ✓ Verified sitters and walkers with clear profiles and ratings.
  • ✓ Secure payments and transparent pricing—no awkward cash hand-offs.
  • ✓ Messaging and updates so you always know how your pet is doing.
Our mission

Make quality pet care simple, safe, and accessible.

We believe every pet deserves consistent, loving care—and every pet parent deserves peace of mind. PawPeer exists to remove the stress from finding help, whether you’re working late, traveling, or just need an extra pair of hands.

  • ✓ Support responsible, well-reviewed local sitters and walkers.
  • ✓ Use smart tools and identity checks to build trust on both sides.
  • ✓ Give pet parents one place to manage bookings, messages, and updates.
How it works

3 simple steps to book trusted pet care.

Whether you’re a pet parent or a sitter, PawPeer keeps things straightforward. No spreadsheets, no endless group chats—just a clear flow from discovery to booking to review.

Step 1
Tell us about your pet & needs

Create a free profile, add your pet’s details, care preferences, and any special instructions. This helps sitters know exactly how to look after your pet.

Step 2
Match with verified sitters & walkers

Browse local sitters and walkers, check their verification badge, ratings, and experience. Chat in-app to confirm fit and ask questions before you book.

Step 3
Book, pay, and stay in the loop

Confirm your booking, pay securely through PawPeer, and receive updates and messages while your pet is in care. When it’s over, leave a review to help the next pet parent.
