About us

A community where pet lovers help each other out.

At PawPeer, we believe that pet care should be as rewarding as it is fun. Our unique subscription-based platform allows pet lovers to connect and swap pet-sitting services, earning redeemable PawPoints along the way. Whether you're looking for a trusted friend to watch your furry companion or want to help out fellow pet owners, PawPeer makes it easy and enjoyable.

Join our community and discover a world where pets are cared for by fellow pet enthusiasts—rooted in sharing, caring and saving with PawPoints.

  • ✓ Subscription-based model that unlocks access to a trusted sharing community.
  • ✓ Swap pet-sitting services and earn PawPoints instead of relying only on cash.
  • ✓ Redeem PawPoints to offset your own pet care costs and support other pet lovers.
Our mission

Turn pet care into a circle of sharing, not just another bill.

Our mission is to build a trusted network where pet parents feel supported and pets are surrounded by real-life friends—not strangers. By rewarding care with PawPoints, we encourage a culture of giving, reciprocity, and community.

  • ✓ Make high-quality pet care more accessible and affordable.
  • ✓ Reward people who show up for pets and their humans with PawPoints.
  • ✓ Build long-term relationships between pet parents, not one-off transactions.
How it works

Share care. Earn PawPoints. Save on your next sit.

PawPeer is designed around simple, fair reciprocity. You help someone’s pet today, you earn PawPoints you can use tomorrow. Your subscription keeps the community safe, verified, and well-supported.

Step 1
Join & set up your pet profile

Choose a plan, create your profile, and add your pet’s details, routines, and preferences. Tell the community how you’re able to help: overnight stays, weekend boarding, quick walks, or check-ins.

Step 2
Share care & earn PawPoints

Offer to look after pets that match your schedule and comfort level. Each completed sit earns you PawPoints—our community currency that recognizes your time, care, and reliability.

Step 3
Redeem PawPoints when you need help

When it’s your turn to travel or take a break, use PawPoints to reduce or cover the cost of your own pet’s care. Share, care and save— all within the same trusted PawPeer circle.
