🛡️ Care Shield Protection

PawPeer Care Shield

Last updated: March 22, 2026

PawPeer offers Care Shield for eligible bookings to provide limited platform support when a covered incident is reported on time and supported with documentation.

What it is

Limited support for eligible incidents during confirmed bookings.

What it is not

Care Shield is not insurance, not a warranty, and not a guarantee of reimbursement.

Users remain responsible for their pets, homes, disclosures, and any losses above Care Shield limits or outside Care Shield eligibility rules.

What Care Shield may help with

  • Eligible minor veterinary expenses tied to a confirmed PawPeer booking
  • Eligible minor property damage reported within the required time window
  • Platform-guided incident review for qualifying reports

Report deadline

Incidents must be reported within 24 hours of the booking end time unless PawPeer states a different deadline in the active Care Shield policy.

What is generally not covered

  • Undisclosed aggression, bite history, medical issues, or other omitted safety information
  • Pre-existing conditions or unrelated veterinary expenses
  • Losses outside the booking window or outside PawPeer's required reporting process
  • Negligence, reckless conduct, criminal acts, fraud, or false claims
  • Amounts above the published Care Shield limit

What users must do

  • Maintain accurate pet profiles and safety disclosures
  • Report the incident through the app or support flow within the required deadline
  • Provide requested evidence, such as photos, receipts, messages, and vet documentation
  • Cooperate with PawPeer during claim review

How review works

PawPeer reviews each submitted claim against the active Care Shield policy, the booking record, messages, disclosures, timestamps, and submitted evidence. PawPeer may approve, partially approve, or deny a claim in its discretion, subject to applicable law and the Care Shield policy in effect at the time of the booking.
