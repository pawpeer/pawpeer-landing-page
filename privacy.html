PawPeer uses account, pet, booking, dispatch, verification, and support information to operate the platform, support trust and safety, and improve user experience.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how PawPeer LLC ("PawPeer," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you access or use pawpeer.io, the PawPeer mobile application, and related services (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, information collected automatically through your use of the Service, and information received from service providers, verification vendors, payment processors, and other users as part of platform operations.

3. Information You Provide Directly

This may include your name, email address, phone number, login credentials, profile details, pet details, booking requests, messages, support inquiries, uploaded photos, verification information, payment-related information, and any other information you submit through the Service.

4. Pet and Safety Information

Because PawPeer is a pet-care coordination and pet-sitting platform, we may collect pet-related information such as pet name, breed, age, weight, vaccination status, medications, medical conditions, bite history, anxiety, reactivity, temperament, compatibility preferences, and other safety-relevant disclosures. We use this information to support matching, bookings, trust and safety, Care Shield review, customer support, and risk reduction.

5. Verification and KYC Information

Where PawPeer requires identity verification, background review, document checks, selfie matching, or similar trust and safety processes, we and our approved vendors may collect identification information, verification status, and related metadata. This information is used for fraud prevention, compliance, trust and safety, platform integrity, payout eligibility, and account review.

6. Location Data and Dispatch Information

If you use real-time dispatch, handoff coordination, walking, sitting, pickup, dropoff, live tracking, or location-enabled features, PawPeer may collect approximate location, precise location, background location, route information, status changes, and device-derived location signals, subject to your device settings and applicable law. We use this information to support dispatch, live updates, route coordination, safety, fraud prevention, incident review, service improvement, and customer support.

7. Device, Usage, and Technical Data

We may automatically collect device identifiers, IP address, operating system, app version, browser type, crash data, referral data, language settings, interaction data, session activity, timestamps, cookies, analytics information, and similar technical information when you use the Service.

8. Payments and Transaction Data

Payments may be processed by third-party providers such as Stripe, Paystack, or M-Pesa depending on region and product configuration. PawPeer may receive transaction metadata such as payment status, booking value, payout status, partial payment details, chargeback signals, and limited billing information needed for operations, support, fraud prevention, and accounting. PawPeer does not store full payment card details except as provided and retained by authorized payment providers.

9. Messages, Communications, and Support

If you message through the platform, contact support, respond to surveys, or communicate with PawPeer by email, SMS, WhatsApp, push notification, or other channels, we may collect and process those communications and related metadata for operations, service delivery, support, trust and safety, dispute resolution, analytics, and compliance.

10. How We Use Information

PawPeer uses information to provide and improve the Service; create and manage accounts; enable bookings, dispatch, messaging, payments, and payouts; personalize user experience; verify identity; support trust and safety processes; evaluate Care Shield claims; detect, prevent, and investigate fraud or abuse; comply with legal obligations; communicate with users; conduct analytics; and enforce our Terms and policies.

11. Data Deletion & Account Closure

You can request deletion of your account and personal information by emailing support@pawpeers.com from the email associated with your account. PawPeer will honor deletion requests as required by applicable law and will notify you when deletion is complete or explain why certain information must be retained, including for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, financial record-keeping, legal compliance, safety investigations, or enforcement of our Terms and policies.

12. How We Share Information

We may share information with other users as needed to facilitate bookings and platform features; with service providers and vendors that support hosting, analytics, verification, payments, messaging, customer support, storage, fraud prevention, and security; with professional advisers; in connection with a corporate transaction; or when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and property. We do not sell your mobile number or personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

13. User-to-User Visibility

Certain profile information, pet details, booking details, ratings, reviews, approximate service status, and communications may be visible to other users as part of the platform experience. Information shared with other users may be retained or further disclosed by those users, and PawPeer is not responsible for independent actions they take with information made available to them through permitted platform features.

14. Cookies, SDKs, and Similar Technologies

PawPeer and its vendors may use cookies, pixels, local storage, software development kits (SDKs), APIs, log files, and similar technologies to remember preferences, authenticate users, understand engagement, analyze product performance, maintain security, and improve the Service.

15. Legal Bases and Consent

Depending on your jurisdiction, PawPeer may rely on consent, contractual necessity, legitimate interests, compliance with law, and other permitted legal bases to process information. Where consent is required for location permissions, marketing communications, or similar features, you may withdraw consent through your device or account settings, subject to legal and operational limits.

16. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain records, support trust and safety, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, and support legitimate business purposes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, applicable law, account status, incident history, payout obligations, and operational needs.

17. Data Security

PawPeer uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and PawPeer cannot guarantee absolute security.

18. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also manage notifications, marketing preferences, and certain permissions through your account, device settings, or by contacting PawPeer. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

19. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 18, and PawPeer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 18 for independent use of the Service.

20. International Transfers

PawPeer may store and process information in the United States and other countries where PawPeer or its service providers operate. By using the Service, you understand that information may be transferred to jurisdictions with different data protection laws than your own.

21. Care Shield, Incident Reviews, and Trust & Safety

If you submit or are involved in an incident, dispute, or Care Shield review, PawPeer may collect and review booking records, messages, profile disclosures, photos, receipts, vet documents, location records, timestamps, and other evidence relevant to evaluating eligibility, platform safety, and policy enforcement.

22. Policy Updates

PawPeer may update this Privacy Policy from time to time by posting the updated version at Privacy Policy. Unless otherwise stated, updates become effective when posted. Continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
