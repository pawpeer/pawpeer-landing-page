PawPeer — Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PawPeer LLC (“PawPeer,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

0. Mobile & Messaging Privacy

Your personal and mobile information is used only for PawPeer LLC’s operational purposes.

This means:

  • We use your information solely to provide, maintain, and improve our services, including messaging related to your account and activity.
  • Your information is not shared, sold, or transferred to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.
  • We do not transfer consumer data to external organizations, even with consent.

When you provide your mobile number and opt in to messaging, you may receive the following types of messages from PawPeer LLC:

  • Customer care/account notifications
  • Account verification codes
  • Security alerts
  • Booking confirmations
  • Booking reminders and updates
  • Service notifications
  • Customer support messages
  • Optional promotional messages and offers related to PawPeer (“promotional messages”)

Message frequency varies based on your account activity (for example, logins, bookings, or support requests). Message and data rates may apply.

To manage messaging:

  • Text HELP for help.
  • Text STOP to unsubscribe from SMS messages. After you send STOP, you may receive a single confirmation message, after which you will no longer receive SMS messages from us, except where required for security or as otherwise permitted by law.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile: name, email, phone, address, pet details, profile photos.
  • Verification (KYC): identity documents, selfies, liveness signals, and similar data processed by vendors (e.g., Persona, Smile ID) where required.
  • Transaction: bookings, payments, subscription status, refunds, receipts.
  • Technical: device identifiers, IP address, cookies, usage logs, crash reports.
  • Communications: messages, support requests, ratings/reviews, and your email, notification, and messaging preferences.
  • Social/OAuth: info you choose to share if you connect third-party accounts (e.g., Facebook Login).

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service.
  • Facilitate bookings, payments, and customer support.
  • Verify identities, enhance trust & safety, and prevent fraud/abuse.
  • Send transactional notices and, with consent or as permitted by law, optional promotional communications.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.
  • Analyze usage to improve features and performance.

3. Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

Where applicable, we process personal data under lawful bases such as: performance of a contract; legitimate interests (e.g., platform security, product improvement); compliance with legal obligations; consent where required.

4. Sharing & Disclosures

We may share information with:

  • Service providers/processors: hosting (Supabase), messaging (Twilio/SendGrid), verification (Persona/Smile ID), payments (Paystack/Stripe/M-Pesa), analytics, and support tools—only as needed to perform services for us and in accordance with our instructions.

We do not transfer your personal data to external organizations under any circumstances, even with consent.

15. Data Deletion Instructions (Facebook Login)

If you signed up or logged in using Facebook Login, you can request deletion of your PawPeer account data by:

  1. Emailing support@pawpeers.com with the subject line "Facebook Data Deletion Request".
  2. Including your full name, the email linked to your Facebook account, and confirmation that you wish to permanently delete your data.
  3. We will verify your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., payment records, fraud prevention).

Alternatively, Facebook users can also remove PawPeer from their connected apps in Facebook settings. Once removed, PawPeer will receive a deletion callback and process your data removal automatically.
