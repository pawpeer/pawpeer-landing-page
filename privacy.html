PawPeer — Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PawPeer LLC (“PawPeer,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

0. Mobile Privacy, Messaging, and Data Use

PawPeer LLC does not share, sell, or transfer your personal information, mobile number, or messaging data with any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

We only use your phone number and messaging data to deliver required transactional notifications, service alerts, verification codes, and messages you have explicitly agreed to receive.

We do not transfer your personal or mobile data to any external organizations under any circumstances, even with your consent. We maintain strict internal policies and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized data sharing.

To opt out of messaging at any time, text STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE.

For help with messaging, text HELP or contact us at support@pawpeer.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile: name, email, phone, address, pet details, profile photos.
  • Verification (KYC): identity documents, selfies, liveness signals, and similar data processed by vendors (e.g., Persona, Smile ID) where required.
  • Transaction: bookings, payments, subscription status, refunds, receipts.
  • Technical: device identifiers, IP address, cookies, usage logs, crash reports.
  • Communications: messages, support requests, ratings/reviews, and email preferences.
  • Social/OAuth: info you choose to share if you connect third-party accounts (e.g., Facebook Login).

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service.
  • Facilitate bookings, payments, and customer support.
  • Verify identities, enhance trust & safety, and prevent fraud/abuse.
  • Send transactional notices and, with consent or as permitted by law, marketing communications.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.
  • Analyze usage to improve features and performance.

2.1 Messaging-Specific Privacy (SMS, WhatsApp, App Notifications)

PawPeer LLC uses your phone number solely to:

  • Send verification codes
  • Send booking confirmations, updates, reminders, and service alerts
  • Deliver support-related communications
  • Send occasional marketing messages only when legally permitted and explicitly opted into

Message frequency may vary depending on your activity.

Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by texting STOP. For help, text HELP.

3. Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

Where applicable, we process personal data under lawful bases such as: performance of a contract; legitimate interests (e.g., platform security, product improvement); compliance with legal obligations; consent where required.

4. Sharing & Disclosures

We may share information with:

  • Service providers/processors: hosting (Supabase), messaging (Twilio/SendGrid), verification (Persona/Smile ID), payments (Paystack/Stripe/M-Pesa), analytics, and support tools—only as needed to perform services for us.
  • Other users: limited profile and booking information necessary to facilitate services between parties.
  • Legal & safety: to comply with law, enforce policies, or protect rights, safety, and property.
  • Business transfers: in connection with merger, sale, or reorganization.

We never share or sell your personal or mobile data with third parties for marketing, lead generation, advertising, or promotional purposes.

We do not transfer consumer data to external organizations under any circumstances—not for marketing, analytics, partnerships, or cross-platform purposes.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and analyze traffic. You can adjust browser settings to refuse cookies; some features may not function properly if you do.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary by data type and legal requirements.

7. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures designed to protect information (e.g., encryption in transit, access controls). No system is 100% secure; you share information at your own risk.

8. International Transfers

We may transfer, store, and process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Your Rights

  • EEA/UK: access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing; withdraw consent where applicable.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): request to know, delete, correct, or limit use/disclosure of personal information; non-discrimination for exercising rights.

Submit requests to support@pawpeer.com. We may ask for verification information.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or other applicable age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us to request deletion.
