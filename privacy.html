PawPeer — Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PawPeer LLC (“PawPeer,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

0. Mobile Privacy, Messaging, and Data Use

PawPeer LLC does not share, sell, or transfer your personal or mobile information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

We only use your phone number and messaging data to deliver necessary transactional notifications, service alerts, verification codes, customer support messages, and occasional promotional messages where permitted by law and where you have opted in, if required.

We do not transfer your personal data to any external organizations under any circumstances, even with your consent. We maintain strict policies and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or sharing of your data.

Opt-Out Instructions: To opt out of future SMS or messaging communications from PawPeer, you can text STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE at any time. For help, you can text HELP or contact us using the information in the Contact section below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile: name, email, phone, address, pet details, profile photos.
  • Verification (KYC): identity documents, selfies, liveness signals, and similar data processed by vendors (e.g., Persona, Smile ID) where required.
  • Transaction: bookings, payments, subscription status, refunds, receipts.
  • Technical: device identifiers, IP address, cookies, usage logs, crash reports.
  • Communications: messages, support requests, ratings/reviews, and email or notification preferences.
  • Social/OAuth: info you choose to share if you connect third-party accounts (e.g., Facebook Login).

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service.
  • Facilitate bookings, payments, and customer support.
  • Verify identities, enhance trust & safety, and prevent fraud/abuse.
  • Send transactional notices and, with consent or as permitted by law, marketing communications.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.
  • Analyze usage to improve features and performance.

2.1 Messaging-Specific Privacy (SMS, WhatsApp, App Notifications)

PawPeer LLC uses your phone number and messaging channels (such as SMS, WhatsApp, and in-app notifications) to:

  • Send account and login verification codes;
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders, and updates;
  • Deliver security alerts and important service notifications;
  • Respond to customer support requests;
  • Send occasional promotional or educational content related to PawPeer LLC, where permitted by law and only if you have opted in where required.

Message frequency varies based on your account activity (for example, number of logins, bookings, or support requests). Message and data rates may apply.

3. Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

Where applicable, we process personal data under lawful bases such as: performance of a contract; legitimate interests (e.g., platform security, product improvement); compliance with legal obligations; consent where required.

4. Sharing & Disclosures

We may share information with:

  • Service providers/processors: hosting (Supabase), messaging (Twilio/SendGrid), verification (Persona/Smile ID), payments (Paystack/Stripe/M-Pesa), analytics, and support tools—only as needed to perform services for us.
  • Other users: limited profile and booking information necessary to facilitate services between parties.
  • Legal & safety: to comply with law, enforce policies, or protect rights, safety, and property.
  • Business transfers: in connection with merger, sale, or reorganization.

We never share or sell your personal or mobile information with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

We do not transfer your personal data to external organizations under any circumstances, even with your consent.

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and analyze traffic. You can adjust browser settings to refuse cookies; some features may not function properly if you do.

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary by data type and legal requirements.

7. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures designed to protect information (e.g., encryption in transit, access controls). No system is 100% secure; you share information at your own risk.

8. International Transfers

We may transfer, store, and process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

15. Data Deletion Instructions (Facebook Login)

If you signed up or logged in using Facebook Login, you can request deletion of your PawPeer account data by:

  1. Emailing support@pawpeers.com with the subject line "Facebook Data Deletion Request".
  2. Including your full name, the email linked to your Facebook account, and confirmation that you wish to permanently delete your data.
  3. We will verify your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., payment records, fraud prevention).

Alternatively, Facebook users can also remove PawPeer from their connected apps in Facebook settings. Once removed, PawPeer will receive a deletion callback and process your data removal automatically.
